# IT Support Onboarding Email — PEC Assist Beta

**Subject:** New toy in Teams — PEC Assist beta 🚀 | Come break it with us

Hey team,

Quick heads up: we've been building **PEC Assist**, an AI bot that hangs out in Teams and turns employee IT requests into real Asana tickets automatically. It reads the message, figures out what's broken and how urgent it is, then drops a task in the **IT Support** project with a fancy title and due date.

You all are hearing about it first because **we need your brains on this**. It's still beta — which means it works, but it's definitely not perfect, and we'd rather fix it with you than guess what you need.

## What we'd love from you

**1. Tear apart the categories**  
The bot currently buckets things into Password Reset, Software Issue, Hardware Issue, Access Request, etc. Does that match reality? Are we missing something obvious? If you're sick of "my email is broken" getting labeled wrong, tell us and we'll retrain it.

**2. Roast the task quality**  
Next time you see an Asana ticket that PEC Assist created, peek at the title and summary. Does it actually describe the problem? Is the urgency way off? Just @ us or reply here — we tweak the AI prompt based on what you flag.

**3. Break it on purpose**  
Post some fake requests in the Teams chat and see what happens. Try weird phrasing, multiple issues in one message, urgent-sounding fluff. We've got a UAT checklist if you want structure, or you can just wing it and tell us what exploded.

## The only rules
- It's beta. Stuff will be weird sometimes. That's the point.
- Your feedback literally changes the next build.
- No such thing as a dumb observation — if it feels off, it probably is.
